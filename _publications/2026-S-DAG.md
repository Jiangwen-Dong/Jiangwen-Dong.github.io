We propose S-DAG, a subject-based directed acyclic graph framework for multi-agent heterogeneous reasoning that enables structured collaboration among diverse LLM agents.

## Framework

<div class="pub-figure">
  <img src="/images/s-dag-fig1.png" alt="S-DAG comparison with prior methods">
  <p class="pub-figure-caption"><span class="pub-figure-label">Figure 1:</span> Prior methods route the entire problem to a single agent based on a coarse domain label. S-DAG conducts fine-grained subject analysis, identifying multiple relevant domains with associated relevance weights, then enables structured multi-agent collaboration over the graph.</p>
</div>

<div class="pub-figure">
  <img src="/images/s-dag-fig2.png" alt="S-DAG framework overview">
  <p class="pub-figure-caption"><span class="pub-figure-label">Figure 2:</span> The S-DAG framework consists of two stages: (1) S-DAG Generation — a GNN predicts relevant subjects and their interdependencies to construct the subject-based DAG; (2) Subject–LLM Matching & Multi-Agent Collaboration — each subject node is matched to an expert LLM and agents collaborate according to the DAG topology.</p>
</div>
