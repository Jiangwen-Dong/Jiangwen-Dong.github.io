I am a PhD student at [The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU)](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/), advised by [Prof. Wanyu Lin](https://www.comp.polyu.edu.hk/~wanyulin/). My research focuses on **graph-driven multi-agent systems** that enable efficient, adaptive, and distributed LLM reasoning across edge–cloud environments.

## Research Interests

My work integrates three core areas:

- **Graph Intelligence** — Structured reasoning via graph-based representations
- **Multi-Agent Collaboration** — Coordinating heterogeneous LLM agents
- **Efficient Distributed Systems** — Edge–cloud computation and adaptive scheduling
